How Ad Detector Works

The process is designed to be simple enough for anyone to use, even without technical expertise.

Tap “Scan Device”

The app retrieves the list of apps that Android allows it to inspect and begins the analysis.

Analyze the Signals

Ad Detector reviews installation dates, overlay permissions, Accessibility Services, auto-start capability, and other technical information.

Review Apps That Need Attention

Results are sorted by suspicion level and clearly explain why each app appears on the list.

Choose What to Do

Users can open app information, disable related permissions, mark an app as safe, or uninstall it.

Important Note Android does not provide an API that can identify with absolute certainty which app generated a specific ad. Ad Detector provides assessments based only on technical signals and does not claim that any app is definitively malicious.
