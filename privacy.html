Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Ad Detector handles data and device permissions.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Ad Detector app developed by TON.

2. Data Processed by the App

To provide app analysis functionality, Ad Detector may process the following information locally:

  • App names, package names, icons, and versions.
  • App installation or update times.
  • Certain permissions and technical components that Android allows the app to inspect, such as whether an app can display over other apps or uses Accessibility.
  • Assessment results and scan history stored on the device.
  • If the user turns on usage access: which apps came to the foreground in the last 30 minutes (package name, screen name, and time). This is used only by the "Just saw an ad?" screen and is not saved.

This information is used to create a list of apps that need attention and explain the reasons for each assessment.

3. Personal Data and Advertising

The current version does not require users to create an account and does not ask for names, phone numbers, addresses, contacts, messages, photos, or personal content.

Ad Detector displays ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and approximate location derived from network information to deliver, measure, and protect advertising. Their processing is governed by their own policies and the consent choices available to the user.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and certain US states), Ad Detector uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent or show privacy choices before ads are personalized. Users can review or change these choices at any time under Settings → Privacy options in the app.

4. Local Data Processing

App analysis is performed on the device. Ad Detector does not upload the scanned app list, scan history, or app usage data to our servers. Internet access is used by the advertising SDK to request and display ads.

5. Permissions

Ad Detector uses the following Android capabilities. Only usage access needs the user to turn it on, and none gives access to personal content:

  • Limited app visibility: Android package queries are used to find launchable apps and apps that declare an Accessibility Service or a boot receiver. Ad Detector does not request the broad QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission.
  • Internet and network state: used to load and display ads through Google Mobile Ads.
  • Hide other apps' overlays (HIDE_OVERLAY_WINDOWS): on Android 12 and later, windows drawn by other apps on top of Ad Detector are hidden while Ad Detector is open, to block overlay ads and tap hijacking on its own screens.
  • Request uninstall (REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES): lets the app open Android's own uninstall confirmation for an app the user chooses. Ad Detector cannot remove any app by itself; Android always asks the user to confirm.
  • Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): optional, and only read after the user turns on "Usage access" for Ad Detector in Android settings. The "Just saw an ad?" screen reads which apps came to the foreground in the last 30 minutes (package name, screen name, and time) to find the app that just showed a full-screen ad. This is processed on the device, is not stored, and is never sent off the device. Users can turn usage access off at any time in Android settings.
  • Quick Settings tile: an optional "Just saw an ad?" button the user can add to Quick Settings. It only opens Ad Detector.
  • System settings links: the app may open relevant Android settings pages so users can review another app's information or permissions.

Ad Detector cannot grant itself permissions or change another app's permissions without user action in Android settings.

6. Third-Party Services

The released version uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertising. Google may collect and process information as described in the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising policies. Ad Detector does not currently integrate Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Scan history and the list of trusted apps (apps the user chooses to hide from scan results) are stored locally. Users can clear scan history in Ad Detector's settings or erase all local data through the app information page in Android settings. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies and user controls.

8. Security

We apply reasonable measures to limit unauthorized access. However, no method of data storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Ad Detector is not designed to collect children's personal information. The app only analyzes technical device information as described in its functionality.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality changes. The latest update date will be displayed at the top of this page.
