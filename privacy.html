Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Ad Detector handles data and device permissions.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Ad Detector app developed by TON.

2. Data Processed by the App

To provide app analysis functionality, Ad Detector may process the following information locally:

  • App names, package names, icons, and versions.
  • App installation or update times.
  • Certain permissions and technical components that Android allows the app to inspect.
  • Assessment results and scan history stored on the device.

This information is used to create a list of apps that need attention and explain the reasons for each assessment.

3. Personal Data

The current version does not require users to create an account and does not actively collect names, phone numbers, addresses, contacts, messages, photos, or personal content.

4. Local Data Processing

App analysis is performed on the device. The list of installed apps is not uploaded to our servers unless a future online feature is added and users are clearly notified before using it.

5. Permissions

Depending on the version and features used, Ad Detector may request or guide users to grant the following permissions:

  • App visibility: used to analyze apps on the device within the limits permitted by Android.
  • Usage Access: used only if the app provides a feature for analyzing recent app activity. Users may decline this permission.
  • Notifications: used to notify users when a scan is complete, with their consent.

Ad Detector cannot grant itself permissions or change another app's permissions without user action in Android settings.

6. Third-Party Services

If the app uses services such as Google Play Services, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google AdMob, those services may process data according to their own policies. The actual list of services must be kept up to date with the released version of the app.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Scan history and the list of apps marked as safe are stored locally. Users can delete app data in Ad Detector's settings or erase all data through the app information page in Android settings.

8. Security

We apply reasonable measures to limit unauthorized access. However, no method of data storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Ad Detector is not designed to collect children's personal information. The app only analyzes technical device information as described in its functionality.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality changes. The latest update date will be displayed at the top of this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact: toandv5@gmail.com.
